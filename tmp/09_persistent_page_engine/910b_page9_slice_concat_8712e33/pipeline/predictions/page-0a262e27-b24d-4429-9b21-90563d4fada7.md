Hence $a_{1}$ is the slope of the secant line passing through the two points $(x_{0}, f(x_{0}))$ and $(x_{1}, f(x_{1}))$.

The coefficients $a_{0}$ and $a_{1}$ are the same for both $P_{1}(x)$ and $P_{2}(x)$. Evaluating (2) at the node $x_{2}$, we find that

 $$ f(x_{2})=P_{2}(x_{2})=a_{0}+a_{1}(x_{2}\quad x_{0})+a_{2}(x_{2}\quad x_{0})(x_{2}\quad x_{1}). $$ 

The values for $a_{0}$ and $a_{1}$ in (9) and (10) can be used in (11) to obtain

 $$ \begin{aligned}{a_{2}}&{{}=\frac{f(x_{2})\quad a_{0}\quad a_{1}(x_{2}\quad x_{0})}{(x_{2}\quad x_{0})(x_{2}\quad x_{1})}}\\ {}&{{}=\left(\frac{f(x_{2})\quad f(x_{0})}{x_{2}\quad x_{0}}\quad\frac{f(x_{1})\quad f(x_{0})}{x_{1}\quad x_{0}}\right)\bigg/(x_{2}\quad x_{1}).}\\ \end{aligned} $$ 

For computational purposes we prefer to write this last quantity as

Definition 4.1 (Divided Differences). The divided differences for a function  $ f(x) $ are defined as follows:

 $$ \begin{aligned}{f[x_{k}]}&{{}=f(x_{k}),}\\ {f[x_{k-1},x_{k}]}&{{}=\frac{f[x_{k}]-f[x_{k-1}]}{x_{k}-x_{k-1}},}\\ {f[x_{k-2},x_{k-1},x_{k}]}&{{}=\frac{f[x_{k-1},x_{k}]-f[x_{k-2},x_{k-1}]}{x_{k}-x_{k-2}},}\\ {3,x_{k-2},x_{k-1},x_{k}]}&{{}=\frac{f[x_{k-2},x_{k-1},x_{k}]-f[x_{k-3},x_{k-2},x_{k-1}]}{x_{k}-x_{k-3}}.}\\ \end{aligned} $$

 $$ f[x_{k\_{j}},x_{k\_{j}+1},\ldots,x_{k}]=\frac{f[x_{k\_{j}+1},\ldots,x_{k}]\quad f[x_{k\_{j}},\ldots,x_{k\_{1}}]}{x_{k\quad x_{k\_{j}}}} $$
