Suitability of Cubic Splines

A practical feature of splines is the minimum of the oscillatory behavior that they possess. Consequently, among all functions \( f(x) \) that are twice continuously differentiable on \([a, b]\) and interpolate a given set of data points \(\{(x_k, y_k)\}_{k=0}^N\), the cubic spline has less wiggle.
