(二) \(n\) 母线系统

\(n\) 母线功率方程是这类方程的一般形式。由式并用结点注入功率代替结点电流后可得

\[
\begin{array}{c} \frac {P _ {i} - j Q _ {i}}{*} = \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} Y _ {i j} U _ {j} \\ \dot {U} _ {i} \end{array} \qquad \quad i = 1, 2, \dots , n
\]

式中 \(P_{i}, Q_{i}\) 分别为结点 \(i\) 的注入有功功率和注入无功功率，因而有

\[
P _ {i} - j Q _ {i} = \stackrel {*} {U} _ {i j = 1} ^ {n} Y _ {i j} U _ {j} \quad i = 1, 2, \dots , n
\]

使 \(\dot{U}_i = U_i e^{j\hat{\delta}_i}, Y_{ij} = G_{ij} + \mathrm{j}B_{ij}\) 则有

\[
\begin{array}{l} P _ {i} - \mathrm{j} Q _ {i} = U _ {i} e ^ {- \mathrm{j} \delta_ {i}} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} (G _ {i j} + \mathrm{j} B _ {i j}) U _ {j} e ^ {\mathrm{j} \delta_ {j}} = U _ {i} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} (G _ {i j} + \mathrm{j} B _ {i j}) U _ {j} e ^ {- (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j})} \\ i = 1 2 \dots , n \\ \end{array}
\]

由于 \(e^{-f(\delta_i - \delta_j)} = \cos (\delta_i - \delta_j) - \mathrm{j}\sin (\delta_i - \delta_j)\)

故有

\[
\begin{array}{l} P _ {i} - j Q _ {i} = U _ {i} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {j} \{G _ {i j} \cos (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) + B _ {i j} \sin (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) \\ + \int [ B _ {i j} \cos (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) - G _ {i j} \sin (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) ] \} \\ i = 1 2 \dots , n \\ \end{array}
\]

将上式的实部、虚部分开则有

\[
\begin{array}{l} \left. \begin{array}{l} P _ {i} = U _ {i} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U [ G _ {i j} \cos (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) + B _ {i j} \sin (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) ] \\ Q _ {i} = - U _ {i} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U [ B _ {i j} \cos (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) - G _ {i j} \sin (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) ] \end{array} \right\} \\ i = 1 2 \dots , n \\ \end{array}
\]

对节点 \(i\) ，

\[
P _ {i} = P _ {G i} - P _ {D i}, Q _ {i} = Q _ {G i} - Q _ {D i}
\]

因而有

\[
\left. \begin{array}{l} f _ {1} = P _ {G 1} - P _ {D 1} - U _ {1} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {j} \left[ G _ {1 j} \cos \left(\delta_ {1} - \delta_ {j}\right) + B _ {1 j} \sin \left(\delta_ {1} - \delta_ {j}\right) \right] = 0 \\ \vdots \\ f _ {n} = P _ {G n} - P _ {D n} - U _ {n} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {j} \left[ G _ {n j} \cos \left(\delta_ {n} - \delta_ {j}\right) + B _ {n j} \sin \left(\delta_ {n} - \delta_ {j}\right) \right] = 0 \\ f _ {n + 1} = Q _ {G 1} - Q _ {D 1} + U _ {1} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {j} \left[ B _ {1 j} \cos \left(\delta_ {1} - \delta_ {j}\right) - G _ {1 j} \sin \left(\delta_ {1} - \delta_ {j}\right) \right] = 0 \\ \vdots \\ f _ {2 n + 1} = Q _ {G n} - Q _ {D n} - U _ {n} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {j} \left[ B _ {n j} \cos \left(\delta_ {n} - \delta_ {j}\right) - G _ {n j} \sin \left(\delta_ {n} - \delta_ {j}\right) \right] = 0 \end{array} \right\}
\]

上式即为 \(n\) 母线系统的极坐标功率方程。如前所述，一个结点有6个变量那么 \(n\) 个结点就有 \(6n\) 个变量，上式只有 \(2n\) 个方程因而必须给定 \(4n\) 个变量方能有解。
