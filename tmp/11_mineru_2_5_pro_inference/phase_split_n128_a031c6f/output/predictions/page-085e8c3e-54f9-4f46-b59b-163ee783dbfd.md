3.2.2 Gamma Distribution

Let \(\Theta_3\) be a subset of \(\mathbb{R}_+ \times (1, \infty) \times \mathbb{R}^{d_3 - 2}\), which is open and convex with a smooth boundary and let \(f_\alpha(y)\) be as follows:

\[
f _ {\alpha} (z) = \frac {1}{\Gamma (\alpha_ {2}) \alpha_ {1} ^ {\alpha_ {2}}} z ^ {\alpha_ {2} - 1} \exp \{- z / \alpha_ {1} \}, \quad (z \geq 0),

for \(\alpha \in \overline{\Theta}_3\). Then,

\[
\psi (x, y, \alpha) = - \log c (x, \alpha) - \log \Gamma (\alpha_ {2}) - \alpha_ {2} \log \alpha_ {1} + (\alpha_ {2} - 1) \log y - \frac {y}{\alpha_ {1}},
