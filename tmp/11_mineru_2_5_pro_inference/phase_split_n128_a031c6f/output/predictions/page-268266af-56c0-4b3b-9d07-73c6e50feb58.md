In the line \((\dagger)\), because of shorter length, we have \(\omega_{|I'| + 3}^{(0)}(q,\tilde{q},u,I') = \omega_{|I'| + 3}^{(0)}(\tilde{q},u,q,I')\) by induction hypothesis. We commute the residues and use the identity (30) for \(z_{2}\mapsto q\) and \(I\mapsto I'\). After relabelling the index sets and commuting the residues again we get for the line \((\dagger)\) exactly the same expression as the line \((\#)\), multiplied by the line \((\S)\), of (31). Similar considerations prove that the line \((\dagger \dagger)\) of (32) is equal to the line \((\#\#\), multiplied by the line \((\S)\), of (31).

The lines \((\ddagger')\) and \((\ddagger'')\) can be combined to a sum over decompositions \(I' \sqcup I'' = I \cup \{u\}\) of \(\omega_{|I'|+3}^{(0)}(q, \tilde{q}, I')\omega_{|I''|+1}^{(0)}(q, I'')\); and the lines \((\ddagger\ddagger')\) and \((\ddagger\ddagger'')\) to a sum over decompositions \(I' \sqcup I'' = I \cup \{\tilde{q}\}\) of \(\omega_{|I'|+3}^{(0)}(q, u, I')\omega_{|I''|+1}^{(0)}(q, I'')\).
