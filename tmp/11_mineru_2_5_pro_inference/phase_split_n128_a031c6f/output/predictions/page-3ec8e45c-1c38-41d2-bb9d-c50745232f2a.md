which was required.

Completeness, Soundness and Entanglement lower bound: Here, we can assume that \(\mathcal{V}\) is \(\lambda\)-bounded. Thus, by Claim 4.49, \(\mathcal{V}_n^{(1)} = \mathfrak{Padding}(\mathcal{V}_n, n^\lambda)\). Then, by Claim 4.50, \(\mathcal{V}_n^{(2)} = \mathfrak{QueRe\mathfrak{d}}(\mathcal{V}_{2^n}^{(1)}, 2^{\lambda n}, \mathcal{B}(\lambda n))\). Finally, by Claim 4.46, \(\mathcal{V}_n^{(3)} = \mathfrak{De}\mathfrak{Type}(\mathcal{V}_n^{(2)})\). Now, as \(\mathcal{V}\) is \(\lambda\)-bounded, \(\mathcal{V}' = (\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{QR}}^{\lambda}, \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{QR}}^{\lambda}, \mathcal{L}', \mathcal{D})\) defines the same games as \(\mathcal{V}^{(3)}\), which means that

\[
\mathcal {V} _ {n} ^ {\prime} = \mathfrak {D e T y p e} \big (\mathfrak {Q u e R e d} (\mathfrak {P a d d i n g} (\mathcal {V} _ {2 ^ {n}}, 2 ^ {\lambda n}), 2 ^ {\lambda n}, \mathscr {B} (\lambda n)) \big).
\]

By Fact 4.48, if \(\mathcal{V}_{2^n}\) has a perfect ZPC strategy, then so does \(\mathfrak{Padding}(\mathcal{V}_{2^n}, 2^{\lambda n})\). Since \(\mathcal{V}\) is \(\lambda\)-bounded, the length of questions in the \(2^n\)-th game \(\mathcal{V}_{2^n}\) (as well as in the padded version) is at most \((2^n)^\lambda = 2^{\lambda n}\), which is the length of vectors returned by \(\mathcal{B}(\lambda n)\). Therefore, we may apply Theorem 4.24 to deduce that

\[
\mathfrak {Q u e R e d} \bigl (\mathfrak {P a d d i n g} \left(\mathcal {V} _ {2 ^ {n}}, 2 ^ {\lambda n}\right), 2 ^ {\lambda n}, \mathscr {B} (\lambda n) \bigr)
\]

has a perfect ZPC strategy as well. Finally, by Corollary 4.42, the detyping of the above has a perfect ZPC strategy, but this is exactly  \( V_{n}^{\prime} \) , proving the completeness requirements.

In the other direction, assume \(\mathrm{val}^*(\mathcal{V}_n') \geq 1 - \varepsilon\). Then, as the typed graph of QueRe\(\mathfrak{d}\), described in Example 4.39 and in Figure 14, contains all self loops, using Corollary 4.43 we can deduce that the value of

\[
\mathfrak {Q u e R e d} \left(\mathfrak {P a d d i n g} \left(\mathcal {V} _ {2 ^ {n}}, 2 ^ {\lambda n}\right), 2 ^ {\lambda n}, \mathscr {B} (\lambda n)\right)
\]

is at least \(1 - O((2h + 28)\cdot 2^{4h + 56}\cdot \sqrt{\varepsilon}) = 1 - O_h(\sqrt{\varepsilon})\) . Furthermore, for entanglement lower bound we have

\[
\mathscr {E} \left(\mathcal {V} _ {n} ^ {\prime}, 1 - \varepsilon\right) \geq \mathscr {E} \left(\text {QueRed} \left(\text {Padding} \left(\mathcal {V} _ {2 ^ {n}}, 2 ^ {\lambda n}\right), 2 ^ {\lambda n}, \mathscr {B} (\lambda n)\right), 1 - O _ {h} (\sqrt {\varepsilon})\right).
\]

By Theorem 4.24, if \(\mathfrak{QueRe\mathfrak{d}}(\mathfrak{Padding}(\mathcal{V}_{2^n}, 2^{\lambda n}), 2^{\lambda n}, \mathcal{B}(\lambda n))\) has value \(1 - O_h(\sqrt{\varepsilon})\), then \(\mathfrak{Padding}(\mathcal{V}_{2^n}, 2^{\lambda n})\) has value \(1 - O_h(h^2 \cdot 2^h \cdot (1 + 2^{2\lambda n}/d^2) \cdot \varepsilon^{1/16}) = 1 - O_h(\varepsilon^{1/16})\), and

\[
1 + ^ {2 ^ {2 \lambda n}} / d ^ {2} \leq 1 + | \mathscr {B} | ^ {2} / d ^ {2} \leq 1 + \delta^ {- 2} = O (1),
\]

as \(d\) was the (un-normalized) distance of the error correcting code of dimension \(2^{\lambda n}\) induced by \(\mathcal{B} = \mathcal{B}(\lambda n)\), and we chose \(\mathcal{B}\) such that its distance is at least \(\delta|\mathcal{B}|\) for a universal constant \(\delta > 0\) (all of this was guaranteed by Fact 3.72). Furthermore, for entanglement lower bounds we have

\[
\mathscr {E} \left(\text {QueRe} \mathscr {d} \left(\text {Padding} \left(\mathcal {V} _ {2 ^ {n}}, 2 ^ {\lambda n}\right), 2 ^ {\lambda n}, \mathscr {B} (\lambda n)\right), 1 - O _ {h} (\sqrt {\varepsilon})\right) \geq \mathscr {E} \left(\text {Padding} \left(\mathcal {V} _ {2 ^ {n}}, 2 ^ {\lambda n}\right), 1 - O _ {h} \left(\varepsilon^ {1 / 1 6}\right)\right).
\]

Since \(\mathcal{V}\) is \(\lambda\)-bounded, \(2^{\lambda n}\) is an upper bound on \(|\mathcal{A}(2^n,\cdot ,\cdot)|\) which is \(\max \{\ell^{\Re},\ell^{\mathfrak{L}}\}\) in \(\mathcal{V}_{2^n}\). Hence, By Fact 4.48, \(\mathcal{V}_{2^n}\) has value \(1 - O_h(\varepsilon^{1 / 16})\), and

\[
\mathscr {E} \left(\mathfrak {P a d d i n g} \left(\mathcal {V} _ {2 ^ {n}}, 2 ^ {\lambda n}\right), 1 - O _ {h} \left(\varepsilon^ {1 / 1 6}\right)\right) \geq \mathscr {E} \left(\mathcal {V} _ {2 ^ {n}}, 1 - O _ {h} \left(\varepsilon^ {1 / 1 6}\right)\right).
\]

Combining all of the above, gives the required soundness and entanglement lower bounds.

By choosing \( c_{\mathrm{QR}}(h) \) (121) to be large enough to bound all the constants along this proof, we conclude the theorem.
