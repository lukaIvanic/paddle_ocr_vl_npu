Definition 3.1. Let \((\mathfrak{M}_{\phi},\rho)\) be a representation of an averaging Lie conformal algebra \((\mathfrak{L},[\cdot_{\lambda}\cdot ]_P)\). Define a map \(\xi^p:C^p (\mathfrak{L},\mathfrak{M})\to C_{AO}^p (\mathfrak{L},\mathfrak{M})\) by

\[
\xi^ {p} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {p - 1}} \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {p}\right) = f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {p - 1}} \left(P x _ {1}, P x _ {2}, \dots , P x _ {p}\right) - (\phi \circ f) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {p - 1}} \left(P x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {p}\right), \tag {15}
\]

for all  \( x_{1},\cdots,x_{p}\in L \)  and  \( f\in C^{p}(\mathfrak{L},\mathfrak{M}) \) .

Lemma 3.2. For any \(x_{1},\dots ,x_{p + 1}\in \mathfrak{L}\) and \(f\in C^p (\mathfrak{L},\mathfrak{M})\) , we have

\[
\xi^ {p + 1} \left(\delta^ {p} (f)\right) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {p + 1}\right) = \partial^ {p} \left(\xi^ {p} (f)\right) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {p + 1}\right). \tag {16}
\]

Proof. First, consider that

\[
\begin{array}{l} \xi^ {p + 1} (\delta^ {p} (f)) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} (x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}, \dots , x _ {p + 1}) \\ = (\delta^ {p} f) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} (P x _ {1}, P x _ {2}, \dots , P x _ {p}, P x _ {p + 1}) \\ - \left(\phi \circ (\delta^ {p} f)\right) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} (P x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {p}, x _ {p + 1}) \\ = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {p + 1} (- 1) ^ {i + 1} \left[ \left(P x _ {i}\right) _ {\lambda_ {i}} f _ {\lambda_ {2}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} \left(P x _ {1}, \dots \hat {P x _ {i}}, \dots , P x _ {p + 1}\right) \right] \\ + \sum_ {1 \leq i <   j \leq p + 1} (- 1) ^ {i + j} f _ {\lambda_ {i} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {j}}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} ([ P x _ {i \lambda_ {i}} P x _ {j} ], P x _ {1}, \dots , \hat {P x _ {i}}, \dots , \hat {P x _ {j}}, \dots , P x _ {p + 1}) \\ - \phi ([ (P x _ {1}) _ {\lambda_ {1}} f _ {\lambda_ {2}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {1}}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} (x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {p + 1}) ] \\ + \sum_ {1 <   j \leq p + 1} (- 1) ^ {1 + j} f _ {\lambda_ {1} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \hat {x} _ {j}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} ([ P x _ {1 \lambda_ {1}} x _ {j} ], x _ {2}, \dots , \hat {x} _ {j}, \dots , x _ {p + 1})) \\ - \phi \left(\sum_ {i = 2} ^ {p + 1} (- 1) ^ {i + 1} \left[ x _ {i \lambda_ {i}} f _ {\lambda_ {2}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \lambda_ {p + 1}} \left(P x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots \hat {x _ {i}}, \dots , x _ {p + 1}\right) \right] \right. \\ + \sum_ {2 \leq i <   j \leq p + 1} (- 1) ^ {i + j} f _ {\lambda_ {i} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {1}}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {j}}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} ([ x _ {i \lambda_ {i}} x _ {j} ], P x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , \hat {x _ {i}}, \dots , \hat {x _ {j}}, \dots , x _ {p + 1})). \\ \end{array}
\]

Next

\[
\begin{array}{l} \partial_ {A O} ^ {p} (\xi^ {p} (f)) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} (x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}, \dots , x _ {p + 1}) \\ = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {p + 1} (- 1) ^ {i + 1} \rho (P (x _ {i})) _ {\lambda_ {i}} (\xi^ {p} f) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} (x _ {1}, \dots , \hat {x _ {i}}, \dots , x _ {p + 1}) \\ + \sum_ {1 \leq i <   j \leq p + 1} (- 1) ^ {i + j} \left(\xi^ {p} f\right) _ {\lambda_ {i} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {j}}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} \left(\left[ P \left(x _ {i}\right) _ {\lambda_ {i}} x _ {j} \right], x _ {1}, \dots , \hat {x _ {i}}, \dots , \hat {x _ {j}}, \dots , x _ {p + 1}\right) \\ = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {p + 1} (- 1) ^ {i + 1} \rho (P (x _ {i})) _ {\lambda_ {i}} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} (P x _ {1}, P x _ {2}, \dots , \hat {P x _ {i}}, \dots , P x _ {p + 1}) \\ - \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {p + 1} (- 1) ^ {i + 1} \rho (P (x _ {i})) _ {\lambda_ {i}} (\phi \circ f) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} (P x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , \hat {x _ {i}}, \dots , x _ {p + 1}) \\ + \sum_ {1 \leq i <   j \leq p + 1} (- 1) ^ {i + j} f _ {\lambda_ {i} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {j}}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} (P [ P (x _ {i}) _ {\lambda_ {i}} x _ {j} ], P x _ {1}, P x _ {2}, \dots \hat {P x _ {i}}, \dots \hat {P x _ {j}}, \dots , P x _ {p}) \\ - \sum_ {1 \leq i <   j \leq p + 1} (- 1) ^ {i + j} (\phi \circ f) _ {\lambda_ {i} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots \hat {\lambda_ {j}}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} \left(P \left[ P \left(x _ {i}\right) _ {\lambda_ {i}} x _ {j} \right], x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots \hat {x _ {i}}, \dots \hat {x _ {j}}, \dots , x _ {p}\right). \\ \end{array}

By using Eq.(7), we see that

\[
\xi^ {p + 1} \left(\delta^ {p} (f)\right) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {p + 1}\right) = \partial^ {p} \left(\xi^ {p} (f)\right) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {p}} \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {p + 1}\right). \tag {17}
\]

By now we complete the proof.
