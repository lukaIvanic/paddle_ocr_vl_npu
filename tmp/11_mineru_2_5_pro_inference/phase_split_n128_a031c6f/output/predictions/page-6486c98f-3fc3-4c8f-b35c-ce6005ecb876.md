Recall the notation \( I_{\xi, m}^{2}(t) = (t, t + \xi) \times (mt, mt + \xi) \) and consider the set

\[

Since we assume that \(\underline{z}_p\) is not periodic, if \(\underline{y} \in \mathcal{W}(p, t)\) then there exists a unique positive integer \(n(\underline{y})\) such that \(\sigma^{n(\underline{y})}(\underline{y}) = \underline{z}_p\). Now, for any \(\epsilon > 0\) define

\[
\mathcal {W} (p, t, \geq \epsilon) := \left\{\underline {{y}} \in \mathcal {W} (p, t) \colon \left| \frac {n (\underline {{y}})}{t} - t _ {m} \right| \geq \epsilon \right\} \quad \text {and} \quad \mathcal {W} (p, t, <   \epsilon) := \left\{\underline {{y}} \in \mathcal {W} (p, t) \colon \left| \frac {n (\underline {{y}})}{t} - t _ {m} \right| <   \epsilon \right\}
\]

Observe that these sets are related to the quantities \( W(n,p,t) \) introduced in Section 4, and in particular

\[
\# \mathcal {W} (p, t) = \sum_ {n \geq 1} W (n, p, t).
\]

We now derive a type of large deviation result which shows that the contribution to the growth of  \( \mathcal{W}(p,t) \)  by the elements y for which  \( n(y)/t \)  is far from  \( t_{m} \)  is negligible.

Lemma 5.3 (Negligible part). Suppose \( m \in S(\mathbf{f}) \) is an admissible slope for \( \mathbf{f} \). For any \( \epsilon > 0 \) there exist constants \( C_2 = C_2(k, m, \xi, \epsilon) \), \( \eta = \eta(k, m, \xi, \epsilon) > 0 \) such that

\[
\sum_ {p \in \Lambda_ {k}} \mathcal {W} (p, t, \geq \epsilon) = \sum_ {p \in \Lambda_ {k}} \sum_ {n: \left| \frac {n}{t} - t _ {m} \right| \geq \epsilon} W (n, p, t) \leq C _ {2} e ^ {t \left(H _ {\mathbf {f}} (m) - \eta\right)}.
\]

Proof. When \( n < k \), the statement follows from a repeated application of Proposition 4.6. Thus, assume \( n \geq k \) and let \( \mathbf{m} = (1, m) \). By Lemma 2.12 we know that the function \( t \mapsto -t\mathbb{P}^*(\frac{\mathbf{m}}{t}) \) is concave down and realizes its maximum at \( t = t_m \). Let \( t^1 = t_m + \epsilon \), \( t^0 = t_m - \epsilon \), and \( \mathbf{z}^i = \nabla \mathbb{P}^*(\frac{\mathbf{m}}{t^i}) \) for \( i = 0, 1 \). By Proposition 2.11, we have

\[
\mathbb {P} (\mathbf {z} ^ {1}) <   0 = \mathbb {P} \left(\nabla \mathbb {P} ^ {*} \left(\frac {\mathbf {m}}{t _ {m}}\right)\right) <   \mathbb {P} (\mathbf {z} ^ {0})
\]

and \(t^i\mathbb{P}(\mathbf{z}^i) - \langle \mathbf{m},\mathbf{z}^i\rangle < H_{\mathbf{f}}(m)\) by Proposition 2.11 and Lemma 3.3.

Consider \( U_{t} = \left(1,1 + \frac{\xi}{t}\right) \times \left(m,m + \frac{\xi}{t}\right) \), so that \( t \cdot U_{t} = I_{\xi, m}^{2}(t) \).
