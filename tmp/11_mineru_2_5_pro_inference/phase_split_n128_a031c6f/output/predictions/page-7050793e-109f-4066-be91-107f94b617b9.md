Next, we show that each relative Rota-Baxter operator induce an anti-Leibniz bialgebra.

Proposition 3.20. Let  \( (A,\cdot) \)  be an anti-Leibniz algebra and  \( (M,\mathrm{l},\mathrm{r}) \)  be a bimodule over  \( (A,\cdot) \) . Let  \( P:M\to A \)  be a linear map which is identified as an element in  \( (A\ltimes M^{*})\otimes(A\ltimes M^{*}) \)  through  \( \mathrm{Hom}(M,A)\cong A\otimes M^{*}\subset(A\ltimes M^{*})\otimes(A\ltimes M^{*}) \) . Then  \( r:=P+\tau(P) \)  is a symmetric solution of the aLYBE in semi-direct product anti-Leibniz algebra  \( A\ltimes M^{*} \)  if and only if P is a relative Rota-Baxter operator of  \( (A,\cdot) \)  associated to  \( (M,\mathrm{l},\mathrm{r}) \) .

Proof. Let  \( \{e_{1}, e_{2}, \cdots, e_{n}\} \)  be a basis of M and  \( \{f_{1}, f_{2}, \cdots, f_{n}\} \)  be its dual basis in  \( M^{*} \) . Then  \( P = \sum_{i=1}^{n} P(e_{i}) \otimes f_{i} \in (A \ltimes M^{*}) \otimes (A \ltimes M^{*}) \) ,  \( r = P - \tau(P) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} (P(e_{i}) \otimes f_{i} + f_{i} \otimes P(e_{i})) \) , and

\[
\begin{array}{l} r _ {1 2} r _ {1 3} = \sum_ {i, j = 1} ^ {n} P (e _ {i}) P (e _ {j}) \otimes f _ {i} \otimes f _ {j} - \mathfrak {l} ^ {*} (P (e _ {i})) (f _ {j}) \otimes f _ {i} \otimes P (e _ {j}) - (\mathfrak {l} ^ {*} - \mathfrak {r} ^ {*}) (P (e _ {j})) (f _ {i}) \otimes P (e _ {i}) \otimes f _ {j}, \\ r _ {1 2} r _ {2 3} = \sum_ {i, j = 1} ^ {n} P (e _ {i}) \otimes (\mathrm{l} ^ {*} - \mathrm{r} ^ {*}) (P (e _ {j})) (f _ {i}) \otimes f _ {j} - f _ {i} \otimes P (e _ {i}) P (e _ {j}) \otimes f _ {j} + f _ {i} \otimes \mathrm{l} ^ {*} (P (e _ {i})) (f _ {j}) \otimes P (e _ {j}), \\ r _ {2 3} r _ {1 2} = \sum_ {i, j = 1} ^ {n} P (e _ {j}) \otimes \mathrm{l} ^ {*} (P (e _ {i})) (f _ {j}) \otimes f _ {i} - f _ {j} \otimes P (e _ {i}) P (e _ {j}) \otimes f _ {i} + f _ {j} \otimes (\mathrm{l} ^ {*} - \mathrm{r} ^ {*}) (P (e _ {j})) (f _ {i}) \otimes P (e _ {i}), \\ r _ {2 3} r _ {1 3} = \sum_ {i, j = 1} ^ {n} - f _ {j} \otimes P \left(e _ {i}\right) \otimes \left(\mathrm{l} ^ {*} - \mathrm{r} ^ {*}\right) \left(P \left(e _ {j}\right)\right) \left(f _ {i}\right) - P \left(e _ {j}\right) \otimes f _ {i} \otimes \mathrm{l} ^ {*} \left(P \left(e _ {i}\right)\right) \left(f _ {j}\right) + f _ {i} \otimes f _ {j} \otimes P \left(e _ {i}\right) P \left(e _ {j}\right). \\ \end{array}
\]

Note that \(\mathfrak{l}^* (P(e_i))(f_j) = \sum_{k = 1}f_j(\mathfrak{l}(P(e_i))(e_k))f_k\), we get

\[
\begin{array}{l} \sum_ {i, j = 1} ^ {n} P (e _ {j}) \otimes \mathrm{l} ^ {*} (P (e _ {i})) (f _ {j}) \otimes f _ {i} = \sum_ {i, j, k = 1} ^ {n} f _ {j} (\mathrm{l} (P (e _ {i})) (e _ {k})) P (e _ {j}) \otimes f _ {k} \otimes f _ {i} \\ = \sum_ {i, j = 1} ^ {n} P \Big (\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} f _ {k} (\mathrm{l} (P (e _ {i})) (e _ {j})) e _ {k} \Big) \otimes f _ {j} \otimes f _ {i} = \sum_ {i, j = 1} ^ {n} P (\mathrm{l} (P (e _ {i})) (e _ {j})) \otimes f _ {j} \otimes f _ {i}. \\ \end{array}
\]

Similarly, we also have \(\sum_{i,j=1}^{n} f_j \otimes \mathfrak{l}^*(P(e_j))(f_i) \otimes P(e_i) = f_j \otimes f_i \otimes P(\mathfrak{l}(P(e_i))(e_j)), \sum_{i,j=1}^{n} P(e_i) \otimes \mathfrak{r}^*(P(e_j))(f_i) \otimes f_j = \sum_{i,j=1}^{n} P(\mathfrak{r}(P(e_j))(e_i)) \otimes f_i \otimes f_j\) and \(\sum_{i,j=1}^{n} f_i \otimes \mathfrak{r}^*(P(e_i))(f_j) \otimes P(e_i) = f_i \otimes f_j \otimes P(\mathfrak{r}(P(e_j))(e_i)).\) Thus

\[
\begin{array}{l} r _ {1 2} r _ {1 3} + r _ {1 2} r _ {2 3} - r _ {2 3} r _ {1 2} - r _ {2 3} r _ {1 3} = \sum_ {i, j = 1} ^ {n} \left(\left(P \left(e _ {i}\right) P \left(e _ {j}\right) - P \left(\mathrm{r} \left(P \left(e _ {j}\right)\right) \left(e _ {i}\right) - P \left(\mathrm{l} \left(P \left(e _ {i}\right)\right) \left(e _ {j}\right)\right)\right) \otimes f _ {i} \otimes f _ {j} \right. \right. \\ \left. + f _ {i} \otimes f _ {j} \otimes \big (P (\mathrm{l} (P (e _ {i})) (e _ {j})) + P (\mathrm{r} (P (e _ {j})) (e _ {i})) - P (e _ {i}) P (e _ {j}) \big)\right). \\ \end{array}
\]

Thus, r is a solution of the aLYBE in  \( A \ltimes M^{*} \)  if and only if P is a relative Rota-Baxter operator of  \( (A, \cdot) \)  associated to  \( (M, l, r) \) . ☐

As a direct conclusion, we have

Corollary 3.21. Let  \( (A,\cdot) \)  be an anti-Leibniz algebra and  \( (M,\mathfrak{l},\mathfrak{r}) \)  be a bimodule over  \( (A,\cdot) \) . If  \( P:M\to A \)  is a relative Rota-Baxter operator of  \( (A,\cdot) \)  associated to  \( (M,\mathfrak{l},\mathfrak{r}) \) , then there is an anti-Leibniz algebra  \( (A\ltimes M^{*},\Delta_{r}) \) , where  \( \Delta_{r} \)  is given by Eq. (3.1) for  \( r=P+\tau(P) \) .
