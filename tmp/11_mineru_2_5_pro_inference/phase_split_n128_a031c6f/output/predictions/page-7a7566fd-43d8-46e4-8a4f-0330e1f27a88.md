Proof. For any \(z_{1}, z_{2} \in \mathbb{C}\), let \(z^{\theta} = (1 - \theta)z_{1} + \theta z_{2}\) and let \(\gamma(\theta) = f(|z^{\theta}|^{2})z^{\theta}\) for \(0 \leq \theta \leq 1\), we have

\[
f (| z _ {2} | ^ {2}) z _ {2} - f (| z _ {1} | ^ {2}) z _ {1} = \gamma (1) - \gamma (0) = \int_ {0} ^ {1} \gamma^ {\prime} (\theta) \mathrm{d} \theta , \tag {3.0.19}
\]

where

\[
\gamma^ {\prime} (\theta) = (1 + \sigma) f (| z ^ {\theta} | ^ {2}) (z _ {2} - z _ {1}) + g (z ^ {\theta}) \overline {{(z _ {2} - z _ {1})}}, \tag {3.0.20}
\]

with

\[
g (z) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} f ^ {\prime} \left(| z | ^ {2}\right) z ^ {2} = \beta \sigma | z | ^ {2 \sigma - 2} z ^ {2}, & z \neq 0, \\ 0, & z = 0, \end{array} \quad z \in \mathbb {C}. \right. \tag {3.0.21}
\]

Then we have, since \(\sigma > 0\),

\[
\(| f (| z _ {2} | ^ {2}) z _ {2} - f (| z _ {1} | ^ {2}) z _ {1} | \leq \int_ {0} ^ {1} | \gamma^ {\prime} (\theta) | \mathrm{d} \theta \lesssim | z _ {\theta} | ^ {2 \sigma} | z _ {1} - z _ {2} | \leq M _ {0} ^ {2 \sigma} | z _ {1} - z _ {2} |, \tag {3.0.22}
\]

which completes the proof.

We say \(F:\mathbb{C}\to \mathbb{C}\) is (globally) \(p\)-Hölder continuous with \(0 < p\leq 1\) if

\[
| F (z _ {1}) - F (z _ {2}) | \leq C | z _ {1} - z _ {2} | ^ {p}, \quad z _ {1}, z _ {2} \in \mathbb {C},
\]

where C is a constant independent of  \( z_{1} \)  and  \( z_{2} \) .

Lemma 3.2. Let \(\frac{\alpha}{4} < \sigma \leq \frac{1}{2}\) and \(F\) be \(2\sigma\)-Hölder continuous. For any \(v \in H_{\mathrm{per}}^{1}(\Omega)\), we have \(F(v) \in H_{\mathrm{per}}^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}(\Omega)\) and

\[
\| F (v) \| _ {H ^ {\frac {\alpha}{2}}} \lesssim \| v \| _ {H ^ {1}} ^ {2 \sigma}. \tag {3.0.23}
\]

Proof. From Lemma 2.1 in (Zhang & Wang, 2024), recalling that  \( \Omega \)  is equipped with the periodic boundary condition, we have

\[
\begin{array}{l} | F (v) | _ {H ^ {\frac {\alpha}{2}}} ^ {2} \lesssim \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {\Omega} \frac {| F (v (x + y)) - F (v (y)) | ^ {2}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \\ \lesssim \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {\Omega} \frac {| v (x + y) - v (y) | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \quad (F \text {is} 2 \sigma - \text {H\"{o} lder continuous}) \\ = \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {\Omega} \frac {\left| \int_ {0} ^ {1} v ^ {\prime} (y + \theta x) x \mathrm{d} \theta \right| ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \\ \leq \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {0} ^ {1} \left| v ^ {\prime} (y + \theta x) \right| ^ {4 \sigma} \mathrm{d} \theta \frac {| x | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \tag {3.0.24} \\ \lesssim \int_ {\Omega} \frac {| x | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \mathrm{d} x \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {0} ^ {1} | v ^ {\prime} (y) | ^ {4 \sigma} \mathrm{d} \theta \mathrm{d} y \quad \text {(change integration order)} \\ = \int_ {\Omega} \frac {| x | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \mathrm{d} x \| v ^ {\prime} \| _ {L ^ {4 \sigma}} ^ {4 \sigma} \\ \lesssim \int_ {\Omega} \frac {| x | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \mathrm{d} x \| v \| _ {H ^ {1}} ^ {4 \sigma} \quad (\sigma \leq \frac {1}{2}) \\ \lesssim \| v \| _ {H ^ {1}} ^ {4 \sigma} \quad (\sigma > \frac {\alpha}{4}, \text {the integral} \int_ {\Omega} \frac {| x | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \mathrm{d} x \text {converges}). \\ \end{array}
\]

Thus, we have completed the proof.
