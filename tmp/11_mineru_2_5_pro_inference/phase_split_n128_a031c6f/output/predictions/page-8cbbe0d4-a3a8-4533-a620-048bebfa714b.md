Proposition 3.4. Let \( n \leq w \), and let \( W(i_1, \ldots, i_n) \) be a wheel in \( \text{conf}(n, w) \). The image of \( W(i_1, \ldots, i_n) \) in \( H_1(\text{conf}(n, w)) \) is

\[

\]

Proof. This follows immediately from the description of the wheels and the fact that the class in first homology corresponding to disk \( i_k \) orbiting disks \( i_1, \ldots, i_{k-1} \) can be expressed as the sum

\[

Concatenation products of wheels behave similarly, and this will allow us to find large disjoint tori in  \( \text{conf}(n, w) \) .
