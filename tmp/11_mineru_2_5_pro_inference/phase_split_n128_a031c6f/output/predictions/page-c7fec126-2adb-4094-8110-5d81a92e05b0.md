For brevity, we bound only  \( E_{j-1}|\overline{\alpha}_{j,2}|^{k} \)  and  \( E_{j-1}|\overline{\alpha}_{j,3}|^{k} \) ; the analysis of  \( \overline{\alpha}_{j,1} \)  and  \( \overline{\alpha}_{j,4} \)  does not differ substantively from the arguments presented below and in Section A.1.

(2) We expand \(\overline{\alpha}_{j,2}\) using the identity \(R_0 = R_{0,-j} - R_0(K_0 - K_{0,-j})R_{0,-j}\):

\[
\begin{array}{l} \overline {{\alpha}} _ {j, 2} = - (\mathbb {E} _ {j} - \mathbb {E} _ {j - 1}) \boldsymbol {u} ^ {\top} \boldsymbol {R} _ {0, - j} \boldsymbol {K} _ {0} ^ {(m)} \boldsymbol {R} _ {0} (\boldsymbol {K} _ {0} - \boldsymbol {K} _ {0, - j}) \boldsymbol {R} _ {0, - j} \boldsymbol {u} \\ = - \left(\mathbb {E} _ {j} - \mathbb {E} _ {j - 1}\right) \left(\bar {\delta} _ {j, 1} + \bar {\delta} _ {j, 2} - \bar {\delta} _ {j, 3}\right), \\ \bar {\delta} _ {j, 1} := \boldsymbol {u} ^ {\top} \boldsymbol {R} _ {0, - j} \boldsymbol {K} _ {0, - j} ^ {(m)} \boldsymbol {R} _ {0, - j} \left(\boldsymbol {K} _ {0} - \boldsymbol {K} _ {0, - j}\right) \boldsymbol {R} _ {0, - j} \boldsymbol {u}, \tag {A.53} \\ \end{array}
