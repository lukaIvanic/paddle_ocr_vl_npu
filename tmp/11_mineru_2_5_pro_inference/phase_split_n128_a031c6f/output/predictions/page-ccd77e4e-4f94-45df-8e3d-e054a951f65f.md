For some  \( x_{i} \notin ((\mu^{-1}(\hat{Y}\tau^{-\varepsilon}))^{-1}, \mu^{-1}(\hat{Y}\tau^{-\varepsilon})) \) , we may as well assume that there exist  \( i_{1} \in \{m_{1}, \cdots, m_{s_{1}}\} \)  and  \( i_{2} \in \{n_{1}, \cdots, n_{s_{2}}\} \)  such that  \( x_{i_{1}} > \mu^{-1}(\hat{Y}\tau^{-\varepsilon}) \)  and  \( x_{i_{2}} < (\mu^{-1}(\hat{Y}\tau^{-\varepsilon}))^{-1} \) , respectively. Let  \( s_{1} := \{m_{1}, \cdots, m_{s_{1}}\} \)  and  \( s_{2} := \{n_{1}, \cdots, n_{s_{2}}\} \) . Then

\[
\langle D f _ {\tau} (x) y, y \rangle + m _ {0} \left| D g _ {\tau} (x) y \right| ^ {2} = \sum_ {i = 1, i \notin s _ {1} \cup s _ {2}} ^ {d} \left(\frac {\partial f (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} ^ {2} + m _ {0} \left| \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac {\partial g ^ {j} (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} \right| ^ {2}\right) + M _ {1} + M _ {2}, \tag {D2}
\]

where

\[
M _ {1} = \sum_ {i = m _ {1}} ^ {m _ {s _ {1}}} \left(\frac {\partial f _ {\tau} (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} ^ {2} + m _ {0} \Big | \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac {\partial g _ {\tau} ^ {j} (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} \Big | ^ {2}\right)
\]

and

\[
M _ {2} = \sum_ {i = n _ {1}} ^ {n _ {s _ {2}}} \left(\frac {\partial f _ {\tau} (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} ^ {2} + m _ {0} \Big | \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac {\partial g _ {\tau} ^ {j} (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} \Big | ^ {2}\right).
\]

We obtain the following estimation of \(M_{1}\) from (D1) that

\[
\begin{array}{l} M _ {1} = \sum_ {i = m _ {1}} ^ {m _ {s _ {1}}} \left(\frac {\partial f (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} ^ {2} \cdot \frac {1}{1 + (x _ {i} - \mu^ {- 1} (\hat {Y} \tau^ {- \varepsilon})) ^ {\bar {\nu}}}\right) \\ \left. + m _ {0} \Big | \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac {\partial g ^ {j} \left(\Phi \left(x _ {1}\right) , \cdots , \Phi \left(x _ {d}\right)\right)}{\partial \Phi \left(x _ {i}\right)} y _ {i} \cdot \frac {1}{1 + \left(x _ {i} - \mu^ {- 1} \left(\hat {Y} \tau^ {- \varepsilon}\right)\right) ^ {\bar {\nu}}} \right| ^ {2}\left. \right) \\ = \sum_ {i = m _ {1}} ^ {m _ {s _ {1}}} \left(\frac {1}{(1 + (x _ {i} - \mu^ {- 1} (\hat {Y} \tau^ {- \varepsilon})) ^ {\bar {\nu}}) ^ {2}} \cdot \left[ \frac {\partial f (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} ^ {2} \right. \right. \\ \left. + m _ {0} \Big | \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac {\partial g ^ {j} (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} \Big | ^ {2} \right] + \frac {(x _ {i} - \mu^ {- 1} (\hat {Y} \tau^ {- \varepsilon})) ^ {\bar {\nu}}}{\left(1 + (x _ {i} - \mu^ {- 1} (\hat {Y} \tau^ {- \varepsilon})) ^ {\bar {\nu}}\right) ^ {2}} \frac {\partial f (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} ^ {2}\left. \right) \\ \leq \sum_ {i = m _ {1}} ^ {m _ {s _ {1}}} \left[ \frac {\partial f \left(\Phi \left(x _ {1}\right) , \cdots , \Phi \left(x _ {d}\right)\right)}{\partial \Phi \left(x _ {i}\right)} y _ {i} ^ {2} + m _ {0} \left| \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac {\partial g _ {j} \left(\Phi \left(x _ {1}\right) , \cdots , \Phi \left(x _ {d}\right)\right)}{\partial \Phi \left(x _ {i}\right)} y _ {i} \right| ^ {2} \right] \\ \leq C \sum_ {i = m _ {1}} ^ {m _ {s _ {1}}} y _ {i} ^ {2}. \tag {D3} \\ \end{array}
\]

For \(M_2\), we analogously obtain

\[
M _ {2} \leq \sum_ {i = n _ {1}} ^ {n _ {s _ {2}}} \left[ \frac {\partial f \left(\Phi \left(x _ {1}\right) , \cdots , \Phi \left(x _ {d}\right)\right)}{\partial \Phi \left(x _ {i}\right)} y _ {i} ^ {2} + m _ {0} \left| \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac {\partial g ^ {j} \left(\Phi \left(x _ {1}\right) , \cdots , \Phi \left(x _ {d}\right)\right)}{\partial \Phi \left(x _ {i}\right)} y _ {i} \right| ^ {2} \right] \leq C \sum_ {i = n _ {1}} ^ {n _ {s _ {2}}} y _ {i} ^ {2}. \tag {D4}
\]

Substituting (D3) and (D4) into (D2) yields that for sufficiently large \(m_0 > 0\) and any \(x, y \in \mathbb{R}^d\),

\[
\langle D f _ {\tau} (x) y, y \rangle + m _ {0} | D g _ {\tau} (x) y | ^ {2} \leq C | y | ^ {2}. \tag {D5}
\]
