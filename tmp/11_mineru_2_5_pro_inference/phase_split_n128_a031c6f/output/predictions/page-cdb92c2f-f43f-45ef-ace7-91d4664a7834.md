SANIA ASIF \( ^{1} \)

Let us consider that  \( (\mathcal{L},[\cdot_{\lambda}\cdot],\alpha) \)  is a Hom-Leibniz conformal algebra, which has coefficients in the representation  \( (l,r,\mathcal{M},\beta) \) . The group of nth-cochain  \( C_{HomL}^{n}(\mathcal{L},M) \)  contains the map  \( f_{\lambda_{1},\cdots,\lambda_{n-1}}:\wedge^{\otimes n}\mathcal{L}\to\mathbb{C}[\lambda_{1},\cdots,\lambda_{n-1}]\otimes\mathcal{M} \)  defined by  \( p_{1}\otimes p_{2}\otimes\cdots\otimes p_{n}\mapsto f_{\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2},\cdots,\lambda_{n-1}}(p_{1},p_{2},\cdots,p_{n}) \) . These maps satisfy the following conformal sesquilinearity condition:

\[
\begin{array}{l} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} (p _ {1}, p _ {2}, \dots , \partial (p _ {i}), \dots , p _ {n}) \\ = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} - \lambda_ {i} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} \left(p _ {1}, p _ {2}, \dots , p _ {n}\right), & i = 1, \dots , n - 1, \\ \left(\partial + \lambda_ {1} + \lambda_ {2} + \dots + \lambda_ {n - 1}\right) f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} \left(p _ {1}, p _ {2}, \dots , p _ {n}\right), & i = n. \end{array} \right. \tag {12} \\ \end{array}
\]

such that \(f\circ \alpha^{\otimes n} = \beta \circ f\). The coboundary map \(\delta_{Hom}:C_{Hom}^n (\mathcal{L},\mathcal{M})\to C_{Hom}^{n + 1}(\mathcal{L},\mathcal{M})\) can be defined by

\[
\begin{array}{l} (\delta_ {H o m L} f) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} (p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}) \\ = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {i + 1} l \left(\alpha^ {n - 1} \left(p _ {i}\right)\right) _ {\lambda_ {i}} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} \left(p _ {1}, \dots , \hat {p _ {i}}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}\right) \tag {13} \\ + (- 1) ^ {n + 1} r (f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} (p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n})) _ {\lambda_ {1} + \dots + \lambda_ {n}} \alpha^ {n - 1} (p _ {n + 1}) \\ + \sum_ {i <   j} (- 1) ^ {i + j} f _ {\lambda_ {i} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {j}}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} ([ p _ {i \lambda_ {i}} p _ {j} ], \alpha (p _ {1}), \dots , \alpha (\hat {p} _ {i}), \dots , \alpha (\hat {p} _ {j}), \dots , \alpha (p _ {n + 1})), \\ \end{array}
\]

where  \(  f \in C_{HomL}^{n}(\mathcal{L}, \mathcal{M})  \)  and  \( p_{1}, p_{2}, \cdots, p_{n}, p_{n+1} \in L \) . With coefficients in the representation  \(  (l, r, \mathcal{M}, \beta)  \) , the corresponding cohomology groups are known as the cohomology of the Hom-Leibniz conformal algebra  \(  (\mathcal{L}, [\cdot_{\lambda} \cdot], \alpha)  \) . If we assume that  \(  l(p)_{\lambda} m = [p_{\lambda} m]  \)  and  \(  r(m)_{\lambda} p = [m_{\lambda} p]  \)  then above equations turns to be

\[
\begin{array}{l} (\delta_ {H o m L} f) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} (p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}) \\ = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {i + 1} \left[ \alpha^ {n - 1} \left(p _ {i}\right) _ {\lambda_ {i}} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} \left(p _ {1}, \dots , \hat {p _ {i}}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}\right) \right] \tag {14} \\ + (- 1) ^ {n + 1} [ f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} (p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n}) _ {\lambda_ {1} + \dots + \lambda_ {n}} \alpha^ {n - 1} (p _ {n + 1}) ] \\ + \sum_ {i <   j} (- 1) ^ {i + j} f _ {\lambda_ {i} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {j}}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} ([ p _ {i \lambda_ {i}} p _ {j} ], \alpha (p _ {1}), \dots , \alpha (\hat {p _ {i}}), \dots , \alpha (\hat {p _ {j}}), \dots , \alpha (p _ {n + 1})), \\ \end{array}
\]
