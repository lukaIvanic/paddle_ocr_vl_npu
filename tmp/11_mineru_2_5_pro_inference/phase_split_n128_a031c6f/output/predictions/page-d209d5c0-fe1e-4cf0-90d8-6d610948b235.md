The following terminology is from [22].

Definition 8.13. A (pointed) free morphism in \(\Xi^{\times}\) is a morphism in the image of the inclusion \(\mathsf{Gr}_{*} \hookrightarrow \Xi^{\times}\). An unpointed free morphism in \(\Xi^{\times}\) is a morphism in the image of \(\mathsf{Gr} \hookrightarrow \mathsf{Gr}_{*} \hookrightarrow \Xi^{\times}\). A refinement morphism \([\Gamma]\) in \(\Xi^{\times}\) is a morphism in \(\Xi^{\times}(\mathcal{G},\mathcal{H})\) with a representative of the form \((\Gamma, id_{\mathcal{H}})\).

So, a free morphism in \(\Xi^{\times}(\mathcal{G},\mathcal{H})\) has a representative of the form \((id,f)\) where \(id\colon \mathsf{el}(\mathcal{G})\to \mathsf{Gr}\) is the trivial identity \(\mathcal{G}\)-shaped graph of graphs with colimit \(\mathcal{G}\) and \(f\in \mathsf{Gr}_{*}(\mathcal{G},\mathcal{H})\). Refinements \([\Gamma]\) in \(\Xi^{\times}(\mathcal{G},\mathcal{H})\) are described by nondegenerate \(\mathcal{G}\)-shaped graph of graphs \(\Gamma\) with colimit \(\mathcal{H}\), and hence induce isomorphisms \(E_0(\mathcal{G})\xrightarrow{\cong} E_0(\mathcal{H})\) on boundaries.

In particular, morphisms in \(\Xi^{\times}(\mathcal{G},\mathcal{H})\) factor as \(\mathcal{G}\xrightarrow{[\Gamma]} \Gamma(\mathcal{G})\xrightarrow{\mathrm{del}_{\setminus W}}\Gamma(\mathcal{G})_{\setminus W}\xrightarrow{f}\mathcal{H}\). Here \(\Gamma\colon\mathrm{el}(\mathcal{G})\to\mathrm{Gr}\) is a nondegenerate \(\mathcal{G}\)-shaped graph of graphs with colimit \(\Gamma(\mathcal{G})\in\mathrm{Gr}\), and \(f\in\mathrm{Gr}(\Gamma(\mathcal{G})_{\setminus W},\mathcal{H})\) is an (unpointed) étale morphism \(\mathrm{Gr}\).
